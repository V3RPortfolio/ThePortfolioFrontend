Distributed AI Processing Architecture
At large scale, security systems must handle massive data streams—millions of logs per minute, terabytes of telemetry each day, and hundreds of thousands of connected devices. Relying on a single centralized AI layer quickly becomes a performance bottleneck.

Jarvis addresses this challenge with distributed AI processing nodes deployed across the environment. This decentralized approach ensures faster decision-making, reduced latency, and scalable intelligence that grows seamlessly with your infrastructure—keeping detection and response continuous, even under extreme load.
